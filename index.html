REPORT

Tailored Financial Analysis & FIRE Plan

Based on KES 15,480 monthly stipend & KES 4,644 monthly investment
  • 🎯
    Utopian FIRE Target at Age 50: KES 165.3 Million

    To support a comfortable lifestyle of KES 100,000/month (today's value) for a family of 5, adjusted for 6% annual inflation over 27 years to KES 482,200/month, using a conservative 3.5% Safe Withdrawal Rate (SWR).

  • 💵
    Option 1: 100% Money Market Fund (MMF)

    Allocating KES 4,644/month. Yields ~10-12% p.a. capital preservation with daily compounding. Highly liquid and safe, but returns vary with interest rates and lacks long-term equity growth to beat inflation.

  • 📊
    Option 2: Diversified Portfolio (Recommended)

    MMF (30% - KES 1,393) for liquidity, SACCO (40% - KES 1,858) for 12-14% dividends & 3x borrowing power, and NSE Equities (30% - KES 1,393) for long-term compound growth. High yield (blended ~13% p.a.) with managed risk.

  • 💡
    Actionable Steps during Stipend Period (Phase 1)

    Maintain KES 50,000 of your KES 173,000 savings in MMF as an emergency fund. Split your KES 4,644 monthly investment: KES 2,000 to SACCO deposits and KES 2,644 to MMF. Prepare CDS & DhowCSD accounts for Phase 2 when your income rises to KES 59,000.

PHASE 1

Foundation (Jul 2026 – Sep 2026)

Stipend period - KES 15,480/month
  • ✅
    Open a Money Market Fund (MMF)

    Start with Cytonn or Britam MMF. Deposit KES 5,000 from savings to begin earning ~10% p.a. with daily compounding. Minimum KES 100 to start.

  • ✅
    Build Emergency Fund

    Target 3 months of expenses (KES 45,000). Your current KES 173,000 savings covers this - keep KES 50,000 liquid in MMF.

  • ✅
    Register on DhowCSD

    Create your CBK Central Depository account now. This is free and prepares you to buy T-Bills and bonds when you have KES 50,000+ to invest.

  • ✅
    Open CDS Account on NSE

    Register on the Nairobi Securities Exchange via Ziidi (M-Pesa) to access stock trading when ready.

PHASE 2

Acceleration (Sep 2026 – Jun 2028)

Job income - KES 59,000/month net
  • 🎯
    Handle immediate purchases

    Sept paycheck: Buy KES 6,995 suit. First paycheck + KES 21,000 top-up → KES 80,000 phone by October 2026.

  • 🎯
    Start weekly KES 350 savings

    From October 2026: KES 1,400/month into MMF. This builds the discipline for larger amounts later.

  • 🎯
    Invest in Treasury Bills

    Once MMF balance reaches KES 50,000, roll KES 50K into 91-day T-Bills (8.5% yield). Keep rolling every quarter.

  • 🎯
    Start NSE stock purchases

    Allocate KES 5,000/month to blue-chips: Safaricom, Equity Group, KCB. Use Ziidi app for single-share trading.

  • 🎯
    Join a SACCO

    Begin with KES 2,000/month shares. By 2030, your SACCO balance can qualify you for a car loan at lower rates.

PHASE 3

Growth (Jun 2028 – Dec 2030)

Career progression - target KES 80K–120K/month
  • 📈
    Scale investments to 30%+ of income

    With higher salary, invest KES 24K–36K/month. Diversify across T-Bonds (5-year), stocks, and SACCO deposits.

  • 📈
    Buy Treasury Bonds (5–10 year)

    Lock in 11–14% yields on bonds maturing in your early 30s. Semi-annual coupons provide income stream.

  • 📈
    Hit KES 1M portfolio by Jan 2030

    Combined MMF + T-Bills + Stocks + SACCO should approach KES 1 million with consistent contributions + growth.

  • 📈
    Purchase first car (KES 1M–1.5M) by end 2030

    Consider SACCO car loan (lower rates) or save + invest difference. Budget KES 10K/month for running costs.

  • 📈
    Move out - rent at KES 40,000/month

    Budget shift: 50% essentials now includes rent. Adjust investment allocation to maintain savings momentum.

PHASE 4

Wealth Building (2031 – 2045)

Family years - wife + 3 children
  • 🏠
    Consider property purchase

    Use accumulated SACCO savings for mortgage deposit. Property ownership eliminates rent and builds equity.

  • 🏠
    Diversify into Real Estate Investment

    Explore D-REITs (like Acorn) or Cytonn structured products for real estate exposure without full property purchase.

  • 🏠
    Max out long-term bonds

    20-year infrastructure bonds at 12–14% with tax exemption. These mature exactly around retirement age.

  • 🏠
    Children's education fund

    Open separate unit trust accounts for each child's education. KES 3,000/child/month from age 0 compounds significantly.

PHASE 5

FIRE Glide Path (2045 – 2052)

De-risk and prepare for retirement
  • 🔥
    Shift to conservative allocation

    Move from 60% equities to 70% bonds/fixed income. Preserve capital as retirement nears.

  • 🔥
    Build 2-year cash buffer

    Keep 24 months of expenses in MMF/T-Bills to weather market downturns in early retirement.

  • 🔥
    Establish passive income streams

    Rental income, bond coupons, stock dividends, and SACCO interest should cover 100% of expenses by age 50.
